Assemblethe impossible

Draft eleven players from different clubs and different seasons. Place them anywhere you like on the pitch — six at the back, five up front, nobody in midfield. Nothing is illegal. The simulation decides what it costs you.

Connecting to engine…

Season-specific

Messi 2010/11 is not Messi 2018/19. Every player is a snapshot of one season, with the attributes that season earned.

Shape has consequences

Your placement produces real structural features — width, cover, build-up — which feed team identity and the matchup before a ball is kicked.

No fake dice

Scorelines come from a Dixon-Coles model driven by expected goals, seeded so the same team and the same seed always replay identically.

The Competition

Champions League

Thirty-six teams. Eight league-phase matches each, then the playoff round, then the knockouts. Every match below is simulated by the same engine that ran your friendly — no shortcuts, no pre-computed tables.

A full season is 144 league matches plus knockouts — this takes a few seconds.
